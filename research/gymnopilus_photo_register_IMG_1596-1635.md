## Modeling assumptions and controls
The 3D field reconstruction is a procedural *interpretation* informed by the above photographs; it is not an image-derived 3D scan. Morphology observed: golden-orange/yellow pilei, crowded pale-tan to cinnamon gills, robust yellowish stipes, torn annular tissue, dense overlapping grouping and autumn grass/leaf litter. Substrate may be buried wood or root material; photos do not establish an exact substrate or host tree. Do not label the ground layer as proof of terrestrial nutrition or specific tree association.
