MycoSim

This is an optional mycology simulation and teaching model, not a specimen record and not a species identification. It establishes the interaction architecture for future specimen-derived fungal models.

Observation, interpretation, identification, and hypothesis remain separate. MycoSim is isolated from specimen data and does not modify, merge, or infer any atlas specimen records.
